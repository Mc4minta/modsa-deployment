Announcement of King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi
on
Multi-intellectuals Scholarship 2024

With gratitude to Her Royal Highness Princess Maha Chakri Sirindhorn who pays attention to education as can be seen from various royal duties in education and to show loyalty to Her Majesty, King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi, therefore, provides scholarships that support students from four neighboring countries, namely Laos, Cambodia, Myanmar, and Vietnam, and was granted the name “Multi-intellectuals Scholarship” which means scholarship of mutual knowledge and started to provide scholarships from the academic year 2011 onwards.

By virtue of Article 8 and Article 13 of the Regulations of King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi on Students Scholarship Fund B.E.2553 and 5/2566 meeting resolution of the Board of Student Education Fund Management on January 29, 2024, it has been agreed to make an announcement as follows:

Article 1. This announcement is called "Announcement of King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi on the Multi-intellectuals Scholarship 2024".

Article 2. This announcement shall be effective from the second semester of the academic year 2023 onwards.

Article 3. Qualifications of eligible applicant:
3.1 is a person with nationality in Laos, Cambodia, Myanmar, or Vietnam.
3.2 has an English language test score that meets the admission criteria as specified by the Program.
3.3 is a person who has been nominated by a Faculty/Institute/Office or have an equivalent status of the university and qualifies to be a student at the university.

Article 4. Number of Scholarship
4.1 It is an annual scholarship with four scholarships for Bachelor's degree and four scholarships for Master's degree.

Article 5. Scholarship Coverage
The scholarship recipient will be supported until the completion of the Program as long as the recipient can maintain conditions as specified in Article 6.
5.1 Tuition fees are actually paid according to the Program. (Tuition fee, credit fee, thesis examination fee)
5.2 Monthly living allowance: Bachelor's degree 8,000 baht, Master's degree 10,000 baht (12 monthly payments per year).

5.3 Health insurance (only inpatient) and accident insurance
   5.3.1 Health insurance (only inpatient) of 1,800 baht per academic year
   5.3.2 Accident insurance of 500 baht per academic year
5.4 Educational equipment rental fee of 10,000 baht per academic year
Please note that all items or only some items of financial support may
be approved and shall be considered according to the suitability of the situation.
Article 6. Scholarship Conditions
6.1 It is a free scholarship. The scholarship recipient must be able to study
throughout the program of study. The recipient must not resign or take a leave
of absence from the study without reasonable causes.
6.2 If the scholarship recipient has any misconduct or misbehaves against the rules
and discipline of the university the recipient is studying at, the scholarship shall
be revoked immediately.
6.3 The scholarship recipient must have a GPA for each academic year as follows:
6.3.1 Bachelor's degree scholarship recipient must have a GPA of at least 2.50 for
an academic year.
6.3.2 Master's degree scholarship recipient must have a GPA of at least 3.25 for
an academic year.
If the scholarship recipient has a GPA lower than the specified criteria, the
scholarship shall be suspended in the next academic year. However, if in the
following semester, the scholarship recipient achieves a GPA that meets the
specified criteria, the scholarship recipient will be considered for resumption of
the scholarship.
6.4 While receiving this scholarship, the recipient must not receive scholarships from
other sources that double the funds received.
Article 7. Chairman of the Board of Student Education Fund Management shall oversee that
all are proceeded in accordance with this announcement. In case there is any conflict
or any circumstance out of this announcement, Chairman of the Board of Student
Education Fund Management's order and consideration on that shall be deemed
final.

Announced on April 5th, 2024
