Announcement of King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi
Scholarships for graduate studies for foreigners from the Greater Mekong Subregion—GMS
B.E. 2568 (A.D. 2025)

**************************************************

King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi would like to provide scholarships to support graduate studies for foreigners from the Greater Mekong Subregion—GMS

By virtue of Article 9 (4) and Article 11 of the Regulations of King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi on Student and Learner Development Education Fund B.E. 2567 and 3/2568 meeting resolution of the Board of Student Education Fund Management on September 9, 2025, it has been agreed to make an announcement as follows:

Article 1 This announcement is called Announcement of King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi in the title of Scholarships for graduate studies for foreigners from the Greater Mekong Subregion—GMS B.E. 2568 (A.D. 2025)

Article 2 This announcement is effective for the Scholarships for graduate studies for foreigners from the Greater Mekong Subregion—GMS B.E. 2568 (A.D. 2025) onward.

Article 3 In this announcement

"University" means King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi

"Board" means The Board of Student and Learner Development Education Fund Management

"Scholarship" means The Scholarships for graduate studies for foreigners from the Greater Mekong Subregion—GMS

"Recipient" means Eligible student who has passed the selection process to receive the Scholarships for graduate studies for foreigners from the Greater Mekong Subregion—GMS

Article 4. Qualifications of Eligible Applicant

4.1 is a resident in the Greater Mekong Subregion (GMS) countries—Cambodia, Myanmar, Laos, Vietnam, and China (only in Yunnan Province).

and 3 academic years for a doctoral degree, provided that the recipient maintains

Article 7. Scholarship conditions
7.1 Scholarship recipients must be able to study throughout the program of study.
Recipients may not resign or take a leave of absence from the study without
reasonable causes.

7.2 If the scholarship recipient has any misconduct or misbehavior that violates the
rules and regulations of the University and/or faculty/institution/office that s/he
is studying in, the scholarship shall be revoked.

7.3 If the scholarship recipient has an educational result lower than the specified
criteria, the scholarship shall be revoked.
7.3.1 Master's degree scholarship recipients must have a GPA of at least 3.25 for
an academic year.
7.3.2 Doctoral degree scholarship recipients must have a GPA of at least 3.50 for
an academic year.
If the scholarship recipient has a GPA lower than the specified criteria, the
scholarship shall be suspended in the next academic year. However, if in the
following semester, the scholarship recipient achieves a GPA that meets the
specified criteria, the scholarship recipient will be considered for resumption of
the scholarship.

7.4 While receiving this scholarship, the recipient must not receive scholarships from
other sources that double the funds received.

Article 8. The scholarship recipient is required to serve approximately 10 hours per week of
student support at the university during the first three semesters of study.

Article 9. The University's decision regarding the scholarship award shall be final.

Article 10. Chairman of the Board of Student Education Fund Management shall oversee that
all are proceeded in accordance with this announcement. In case there is any
conflict or any circumstance out of this announcement, Chairman of the Board of
Student Education Fund Management's order and consideration on that shall be
deemed final.
