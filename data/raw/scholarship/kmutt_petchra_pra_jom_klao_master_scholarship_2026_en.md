Announcement of King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi
The Petchra Pra Jom Klao Master Scholarship
B.E. 2569 (A.D. 2026)

The Petchra Pra Jom Klao Scholarship has been established by King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi since 2004 to provide financial support to both undergraduate and graduate students, who are competent, have potential and eager to learn, since 2004 (2547) academic year up until now.

By virtue of Article 9 (4) and Article 11 of the Regulations of King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi on Student and Learner Development Education Fund B.E.2567 and 1/2568 meeting resolution of the Board of Student and Learner Development Education Fund Management on April 9, 2025, it has been agreed to make an announcement of the Petchra Pra Jom Klao Master Scholarship.

Article 1 This announcement is called “Announcement of King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi: The Petchra Pra Jom Klao Master Scholarship B.E. 2569 (A.D. 2026)”.

Article 2 This announcement is effective for The Petchra Pra Jom Klao Master Scholarship students who have studied from academic year 2569 (2026) onward.

Article 3 In this announcement,

"University" means King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi

"Board" means the Board of Student Education Fund Management

"Scholarship" means Petchra Pra Jom Klao Master Scholarship

"Recipient" means eligible student who has passed the selection process to receive the Petchra Pha Jom Klao Master Scholarship

Article 4 Objective of the Scholarship: To support graduate students, who are competent, have potential and eager to learn, with a scholarship so that they are able to study all through the entire program and in accordance with the set conditions.

Article 7 Application and Selection
7.1 Applying in person
7.2 Being nominated by any committee of the university, faculties or departments
7.3 Being co-nominated by the university in cooperation with the government sector's
or private sector's organizations.

An applicant intending to apply for the scholarship must firstly enroll for
the university's master's degree program according to the set application and
selection schedule.

For Program Support Scholarship, eligible student shall be selected and
nominated by the department. An applicant can fill in the application form for
the Petchra Pra Jom Klao Master Scholarship at the Admission and Recruitment
Office website https://join.kmutt.ac.th/en attaching necessary document as
required

For Research Group Support Scholarship, eligible student shall be selected
and nominated by the Research, Innovation and Partnerships Office. An applicant
can fill in the application form for the Petchra Pra Jom Klao Master Scholarship at
the Admission and Recruitment Office website https://join.kmutt.ac.th/en
attaching necessary document as required.

Article 8 Conditions for Receiving Scholarship
8.1 The scholarship recipient must complete signing both the scholarship and the
guarantee contracts.
8.2 The scholarship will be covered only for the duration of the study program and
cannot be extended. Should the scholarship recipient complete his/her study
before the set program period, it shall be deemed end of funding.
8.3 The university shall consider to continue granting the scholarship to the recipient
on yearly basis based on the following criteria:
(1) The scholarship recipient must have GPAX of not less than 3.25 at the end
of academic year. However, in case of non-subject course, the scholarship
recipient must achieve “Good” for his/her research progress. For scholarship
recipient whose GPAX or research progress less than those specified but can
acquire as per the criteria for the following semester, he/she may be
considered to continue receiving the scholarship.
