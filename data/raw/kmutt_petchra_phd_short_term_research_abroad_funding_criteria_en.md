Announcement of King Mongkut’s University of Technology Thonburi
Criteria and Guidelines for Funding to Support Short-term Research Abroad
for Petchra Pra Jom Klao Ph.D. Research Scholarship
*********************************

In order to encourage and support distinguished Petchra Pra Jom Klao Ph.D. Research Scholarship, by virtue of the power under Section 9 (4) of King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi regulations on Education Fund for Students and Learners, B.E. 2567 (2024) in conjunction with the resolution of the Board of Student and Learner Development Education Fund Management at the $4^{\text{th}}/2025$ meeting on 4 December 2025, it is deemed to issue the following announcement:

Article 1. This announcement is called King Mongkut’s University of Technology Thonburi Announcement on Criteria and Guidelines for Funding to Support Short-term Research Abroad for Petchra Pra Jom Klao Ph.D. Research Scholarship.

Article 2. This announcement is applicable to Petchra Pra Jom Klao Ph.D. Research Scholarship from Academic year 2025 onward.

Article 3. In this announcement:

| "University" | means | King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Committee" | means | The board of Student and Learner Development Education Fund Management |
| "Funding" | means | Funding to support short-term research abroad for Petchra Pra Jom Klao Ph.D. Research Scholarship |
| "Students" | means | Petchra Pra Jom Klao Ph.D. Research Scholarship students who are selected to receive Funding for short-term research abroad |

Article 4. Objectives of Funding
4.1 To provide financial assistance for distinguished students seeking to pursue short-term research opportunities abroad.
4.2 To establish research networks and doctoral-level collaborations with foreign countries.

Article 5. Qualifications of Students Eligible for Applying for Funding:
5.1 General Qualifications:
(1) Being a Petchra Pra Jom Klao Ph.D. Research Scholarship recipient currently enrolled in the following programs:

Article 6. Application for Funding
6.1 The student is required to plan for the short-term research abroad with the
principal advisor and the advisor abroad. Additionally, a letter of acceptance
from the host advisor (abroad) is required to confirm the visit. The details of
the timeframe and duration of the trip are as follows:

| Program | Application Period | Permitted Travel Time<br>(Round Trip) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **3-year Program** | Semester 2 to 4 | Semester 3 to 5 |
| **4-year program** | Semester 3 to 6 | Semester 4 to 7 |
| **5-year program** | Semester 4 to 8 | Semester 5 to 9 |

Students must conclude their short-term research trip prior to the expiration of the
scholarship period in the final semester of the program.
6.2 Download Form P.05, an Application Form for Short-term Research Abroad
Grant and Budgetary Approval, at https://www.kmutt.ac.th/sfa/ website and
attach the following documents:
(1) Form P.06, a Nomination Form and Collaboration Plan with an overseas advisor.
The form can be downloaded at https://www.kmutt.ac.th/sfa/ website.
(2) Letter of Acceptance from the overseas advisor confirming the research visit
(3) Research Plan Abroad (not exceeding one A4 page)
(4) Copy of Qualifying Examination (QE) results

Article 7. The student selection process will be based on the following criteria:
7.1 Academic performance and progress in Doctoral studies.
7.2 The objectives and research plan are clear as per no. 5 and no. 6 of the Funding
Announcement and in line with the research title and the expertise of the
overseas advisor as well as the possibility of research plan and duration.
7.3 Personality, ethics, responsibility, leadership and appropriate attitudes for
representing the University.
Articles 8. A maximum of 10 scholarships are awarded per academic year, distributed in two
cycles with a limit of 5 scholarships per intake.

Article 10. The Research, Innovation and Partnerships Office will reimburse the advisor for the following expenses:
10.1 A one-time travel allowance is provided for the principal advisor or co-advisor to visit the student and engage in an academic exchange program during the student's research abroad, for a maximum duration of 7 days.
(1) Allowances and accommodation expenses are provided per University regulations.
(2) Actual costs for economy airfare, passport, visa, and comprehensive insurance (travel, health, and accident), capped at 65,000 Baht in total.
10.2 Expenses for a one-time follow-up visit by the overseas advisor (not exceeding 7 days). The advisor's profile and achievements must be submitted for nomination.
(1) The advisor shall receive compensation at a rate of 7,000 Baht per day, inclusive of allowances and accommodation.
(2) Actual costs for economy airfare, passport, visa, and comprehensive insurance (travel, health, and accident), capped at 65,000 Baht in total.

Article 11. Funding Cancellation
11.1 The student is either qualified for graduation or has a necessary reason for academic withdrawal.

Article 12 Funding Conditions for Short-term Research Abroad:
12.1 Students must complete both the funding and guarantee agreements.
12.2 Students must submit the research report within one month of returning from a
short-term research trip abroad.
12.3 Students are required to submit one (1) full research article derived from their thesis
conducted abroad. The manuscript must be an original work—not a compilation
from conference proceedings—and must be published or officially accepted for
publication in an international journal indexed in the Web of Science database prior
to graduation. However, the student must be the first author and must state
“Petchra Pra Jom Klao Doctoral Research Scholarship from King Mongkut’ s
University of Technology Thonburi”in the acknowledgements.

Article 13. Should the student withdraw from, abandon, or be dismissed from the Doctoral
program, fail to complete their studies for any reason, or terminate the enrollment
without prior approval from the Board of Student and Learner Development
Education Fund Management, the student must submit a request for the
reimbursement of financial support (Form P.13). This also applies in cases of
disciplinary misconduct, inappropriate behavior regarding short-term research
Funding, or any breach of this agreement. The aforementioned form must be
submitted to the Student Financial Aid Unit within 30 days. The student agrees to
repay the full amount of the funds received from the University within 30 days of
the notification date. If the student fails to pay the said amount in full within the
specified period, the student agrees to be charged interest at the rate of 5% (five
percent) per annum, calculated from the day after the due date until the debt is
fully paid.

In the event that the student submits a request to pay the repayment amount in
installments as per the preceding paragraph, the decision shall be at the discretion
of the University and in accordance with its criteria.

Article 14. Student shall be exempt from liability in the following cases:
14.1 Death
14.2 Sickness, with the following 7 severe diseases: Non-invasive cancer, Invasive
cancer, Major Stroke, Chronic Kidney Failure, Severe Chronic Obstructive
Pulmonary Disease / End-stage Lung Disease, Acute Heart Attack and
Fulminant Viral Hepatitis.
14.3 Disabled
14.4 Mental infirmity
14.5 Mental unsoundness or legal incapacity
